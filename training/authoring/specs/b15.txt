DOMAIN: polar research station resupply
GROUP: science
PARAM: STATION TYPE
- Coastal station with a ship berth :: an ice-free approach in summer :: bulk resupply by sea, dependence on a short shipping window
- Deep field inland camp :: air support and a prepared skiway :: science far from the coast, everything flown in
- Summer-only field hut :: seasonal occupation only :: minimal infrastructure, no winter capability at all
- Year-round wintering base :: fuel and food for a full winter :: continuous occupation, no resupply for eight months
PARAM: RESUPPLY MEANS
- Ice-strengthened ship :: an open water or navigable ice route :: very large tonnage in one delivery, limitation to the summer window
- Wheeled heavy aircraft :: a blue ice or prepared hard runway :: rapid delivery of people and priority cargo, a strict runway requirement
- Ski-equipped light aircraft :: a snow surface and good visibility :: access to almost anywhere, a small payload
- Overland traverse :: tractors and a crevasse-surveyed route :: heavy fuel delivered inland, weeks of travel each way
PARAM: LIMITING CONDITION
- Sea ice failing to break out :: a heavy ice year :: no ship access to the station, cargo carried over to next season
- Persistent whiteout :: no visible horizon for days :: no aircraft movement of any kind, ground work continuing
- Fuel shortfall at the station :: consumption above plan :: reduced generator hours, science curtailed
- Medical evacuation required :: a casualty needing surgery :: an unplanned flight demanded, risk accepted outside normal limits
PARAM: SEASON OUTCOME
- Full science programme delivered :: people and cargo arriving as planned :: all projects supported, the station left well found
- Programme reduced to essentials :: partial resupply only :: station keeping only, science deferred a year
- Station placed on care and maintenance :: insufficient support to operate :: no occupation, systems winterised
- Emergency winter resupply mounted :: a failure that cannot wait for spring :: extraordinary cost, a flight at the edge of capability

DOMAIN: patent infringement litigation
GROUP: legal
PARAM: PATENT POSITION
- Granted patent in the jurisdiction :: examination completed and fees paid :: enforceable rights, a presumption of validity
- Pending application only :: a filing made and examination incomplete :: no enforceable right yet, protection only on later grant
- Patent expired or lapsed :: the term ended or renewal missed :: no exclusivity remaining, the disclosure free for anyone to use
- Patent already revoked on prior art :: a validity challenge already succeeded :: no rights at all, the invention in the public domain
PARAM: ALLEGED INFRINGEMENT
- Literal copying of a claim :: a product falling squarely within the wording :: a straightforward comparison, little construction needed
- Equivalents argument :: a variant outside the literal wording :: dependence on the doctrine applied, an uncertain outcome
- Contributory supply of a component :: a part with no other substantial use :: liability without making the whole, dependence on the supplier's knowledge
- Independent development :: a defendant who never saw the patent :: no copying to prove, intent irrelevant to infringement
PARAM: DEFENCE STRATEGY
- Invalidity counterclaim :: prior art located and pleaded :: an attack on the patent itself, a risk of losing the patent entirely
- Non-infringement on construction :: an arguable claim interpretation :: no challenge to validity, a narrow escape on wording
- Licence already held :: an existing agreement covering the acts :: a complete defence, no need to litigate the merits
- Settlement and cross-licence :: a portfolio of value to the other side :: a business resolution, no judgment on the merits
PARAM: LITIGATION OUTCOME
- Injunction granted :: infringement of a valid claim found :: the product off the market, damages to follow
- Patent revoked in the proceedings :: invalidity established at trial :: no rights left to enforce, costs against the patentee
- Claim dismissed for non-infringement :: the product held outside the claims :: the patent surviving, the defendant free
- Confidential settlement :: agreement reached before judgment :: no public ruling, terms unknown
