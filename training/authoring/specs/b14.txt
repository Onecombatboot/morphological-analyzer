PARAM: COASTLINE TYPE
- Soft cliff of clay and sand :: an unconsolidated cliff face :: rapid retreat measured in metres per year, sediment supplied to beaches downdrift
- Hard rock headland :: resistant bedrock at the shore :: negligible retreat within a human lifetime, no sediment released
- Barrier beach and dune :: a mobile sand system :: natural rollback landward, protection only while the dune survives
- Estuarine mudflat and marsh :: a low energy tidal environment :: sediment accumulation rather than loss, no exposure to open ocean waves
PARAM: INTERVENTION
- Hard sea wall :: capital funding and a maintainable structure :: fixed defence of the line behind it, loss of the beach in front over time
- Beach nourishment :: a compatible sediment source and repeat funding :: preserved beach and amenity, recurring cost every few years
- Managed realignment :: land that can be given up :: restored intertidal habitat, permanent loss of the land surrendered
- No active intervention :: a decision to let the coast adjust :: natural processes continuing, no protection of assets at all
PARAM: ASSET AT RISK
- Dense town frontage :: buildings immediately behind the shore :: very high value at stake, no room to retreat inland
- Single coastal road :: a route with no alternative :: severance of access if lost, moderate replacement cost
- Farmland of low value :: agricultural land behind the shore :: limited economic loss, space available to give ground
- No built assets present :: an undeveloped shoreline :: nothing to defend, no economic case for works
PARAM: FUNDING OUTCOME
- Scheme funded and built :: a benefit-cost case that passes :: works constructed, a defined design life
- Scheme refused on cost-benefit :: benefits below the cost threshold :: no works, the shoreline left as it is
- Community-funded partial works :: local contribution raised :: a reduced scheme, protection of part of the frontage
- Defence abandoned at end of life :: an existing structure past its design life :: withdrawal of maintenance, reversion to natural processes
