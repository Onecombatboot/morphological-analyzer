DOMAIN: livestock disease outbreak
GROUP: agriculture
PARAM: PATHOGEN
- Highly contagious viral disease :: airborne or contact spread between herds :: spread over kilometres without any movement of animals, no treatment, statutory notifiable status
- Vector-borne viral disease :: a competent insect vector and a season :: spread bounded by vector range, no direct animal-to-animal transmission, seasonal cessation
- Bacterial disease treatable with antimicrobials :: bacterial aetiology :: treatment available, production losses rather than mortality, no need to cull
- Prion disease with a long incubation :: contaminated feed years earlier :: no live test at all, cases appearing years after exposure, no transmission between living animals
PARAM: HOLDING TYPE
- Intensive indoor unit :: housed animals at high density :: complete records, biosecurity feasible, catastrophic loss if breached
- Extensive upland grazing :: animals dispersed over open hill :: no possibility of housing, contact with wildlife, gathering takes days
- Smallholder mixed farm :: a few animals alongside crops :: informal records, animals moved to local markets, limited biosecurity knowledge
- Live animal market :: animals from many holdings mixed briefly :: enormous contact network in one day, no ownership continuity
PARAM: CONTROL MEASURE
- Movement standstill :: legal power and enforcement capacity :: immediate halt to spread by transport, severe welfare and trade consequences
- Ring vaccination around cases :: an effective vaccine and cold chain :: protection of a zone within days, loss of disease-free trading status
- Cull of infected and contact herds :: valuation, slaughter and disposal capacity :: removal of the reservoir, compensation cost and public opposition
- Enhanced surveillance only :: a testing programme and laboratory capacity :: detection of spread, no reduction in transmission whatsoever
PARAM: OUTCOME
- Contained at the index holding :: rapid detection and effective restriction :: minimal loss, disease-free status recovered quickly
- Regional epidemic :: spread beyond the initial zone before detection :: mass culling, months of restriction
- Endemic establishment :: the pathogen persisting in the population :: permanent change to husbandry and trade
- Export market closure :: trading partners suspending imports :: economic loss far exceeding the animal loss

DOMAIN: antimicrobial resistance outbreak in a hospital
GROUP: healthcare
PARAM: ORGANISM
- Carbapenem-resistant enterobacteria :: gut colonisation and selection pressure :: silent carriage for months, spread by hands and by sinks, very few treatment options
- Meticillin-resistant staphylococcus :: skin and nasal colonisation :: decolonisation possible, spread by direct contact, established screening exists
- Clostridioides difficile :: disruption of gut flora by antibiotics :: spore survival on surfaces for months, defeated only by sporicidal cleaning
- Multi-resistant fungal species :: environmental persistence on surfaces and skin :: survival of routine disinfection, no decolonisation regimen at all
PARAM: TRANSMISSION ROUTE
- Hands of staff :: direct contact between patient and staff :: interruptible by hand hygiene alone, the commonest route by far
- Contaminated environment and equipment :: organism surviving on surfaces :: independent of any individual staff behaviour, reservoir persisting between patients
- Wastewater plumbing :: sink traps and drains harbouring biofilm :: continuous reintroduction, no cleaning regimen reaching it, engineering works required
- Patient transfer between units :: movement of a colonised patient :: seeding of a new ward, detectable by admission screening
PARAM: CONTROL MEASURE
- Hand hygiene campaign :: staff engagement and product availability :: reduction of contact spread, no effect on an environmental reservoir
- Contact precautions and isolation :: single rooms and enough of them :: separation of colonised patients, limited by room availability
- Sporicidal terminal cleaning :: an agent active against spores and time to apply it :: removal of surface reservoir, no effect on carriage in a patient
- Antimicrobial stewardship :: prescriber cooperation and pharmacy data :: reduction of selection pressure over months, no effect on transmission already under way
PARAM: OUTCOME
- Single case, no transmission :: colonisation identified and contained :: no outbreak
- Ward outbreak controlled :: transmission stopped within one unit :: ward closure and deep clean, no wider spread
- Hospital-wide endemic spread :: the organism established across units :: permanent screening burden, elective activity constrained
- Unit closure to admissions :: transmission uncontrolled despite measures :: loss of capacity, transfer of pressure to other hospitals

GROUP: natural hazard
PARAM: SOLAR EVENT
- X-ray flare :: a solar flare on the visible disc :: effect at the speed of light with no warning at all, minutes of high-frequency radio blackout
- Solar energetic particle event :: particles accelerated toward the earth :: arrival within tens of minutes, radiation hazard at altitude, no ground currents
- Coronal mass ejection :: an ejection directed at the earth :: one to three days of transit warning, geomagnetically induced currents at ground level
- Solar radio burst :: broadband radio emission from the sun :: interference on the sunlit hemisphere only, no particle or magnetic effect
PARAM: AFFECTED SYSTEM
- High-voltage transformer :: a long conductor grounded at both ends :: saturation and heating from induced currents, months to replace a failed unit
- Satellite in orbit :: a spacecraft outside the atmosphere :: charging and single-event upsets, no shielding by the atmosphere
- Polar aviation route :: flights at high latitude and altitude :: crew radiation dose and loss of high-frequency communication, reroutable at a fuel cost
- Precision positioning service :: users depending on ionospheric corrections :: degraded accuracy from ionospheric disturbance, no hardware damage
PARAM: WARNING AND MITIGATION
- Forecast from a solar observatory :: a spacecraft at the sun-earth line :: fifteen to sixty minutes of warning of arrival, no warning for light-speed effects
- Grid operator load reconfiguration :: hours of notice and switching capability :: reduced induced current path, loss of transfer capability
- Satellite safe mode :: onboard autonomy and a command link :: protection of the payload, loss of service during the event
- Reroute aircraft to lower latitudes :: fuel and airspace availability :: dose reduction, longer flights and schedule disruption
PARAM: CONSEQUENCE
- No operational effect :: the event below the threshold for any system :: no consequence
- Temporary service degradation :: effects lasting hours :: reduced accuracy or availability, full recovery
- Permanent equipment damage :: hardware stressed beyond tolerance :: replacement required, long outage
- Cascading grid disturbance :: protection operating on distorted waveforms :: wide-area loss of supply

DOMAIN: landslide in a hillside settlement
GROUP: natural hazard
PARAM: FAILURE MECHANISM
- Rainfall-triggered shallow slide :: prolonged or intense rainfall on a soil mantle :: failure within hours of the rain, thin fast-moving debris, seasonal timing
- Deep-seated rotational slump :: a weak layer at depth :: movement of centimetres per year with occasional acceleration, whole hillsides involved
- Undercutting by a river :: a watercourse eroding the toe :: progressive loss of support, failure at the toe first, predictable location
- Slope loaded by construction fill :: material placed on an unstable slope :: an entirely human-made cause, failure soon after placement
PARAM: SETTLEMENT PATTERN
- Informal housing on the slope :: unregulated construction on steep ground :: no engineering of any kind, cut-and-fill terracing by residents, very high exposure
- Engineered terraces with drainage :: designed slope works :: managed groundwater, documented design life
- Housing at the toe of the slope :: development on the runout apron :: exposure to debris rather than to ground movement, no influence on the slope itself
- Housing above the crest :: development on the plateau :: exposure to retrogressive failure, ground cracking as an early sign
PARAM: WARNING INDICATOR
- Rainfall threshold exceedance :: a rain gauge network and a local threshold :: hours of lead time, applicable only to rainfall-triggered failure
- Ground movement instrumentation :: inclinometers or survey markers installed :: detection of slow movement, useless for a failure that begins suddenly
- Visible tension cracking :: cracks large enough to be noticed :: unambiguous but very late, minutes to days
- No indicator at all :: no monitoring and no visible sign :: no warning whatsoever
PARAM: RISK REDUCTION
- Slope drainage works :: engineering access and a groundwater problem :: reduction of pore pressure, no effect on undercutting by water at the toe
- Toe protection and revetment :: river access and construction capacity :: prevention of undercutting, no effect on rainfall infiltration
- Managed relocation of residents :: land elsewhere and consent :: complete removal of exposure, social and political cost
- Early warning and evacuation :: a warning indicator and a rehearsed route :: lives saved, no protection of any property

DOMAIN: urban air pollution episode
GROUP: environment
PARAM: POLLUTANT
- Traffic nitrogen dioxide :: vehicle exhaust in a street canyon :: concentrations highest beside the kerb, falling away within tens of metres, weekday peaks
- Secondary particulate :: precursors reacting downwind over hours :: uniform concentrations across a whole city, no local source to control
- Wood smoke from domestic heating :: solid fuel burning in dwellings :: evening and overnight peaks, worst in cold still weather, residential rather than roadside
- Industrial plume grounding :: a stack plume brought to the surface :: a narrow downwind sector, episodic and short, a single identifiable operator
PARAM: METEOROLOGY
- Temperature inversion :: a stable layer trapping air near the ground :: no vertical dispersion, accumulation over days
- Strong wind :: rapid horizontal transport :: dilution of everything local, possible import from elsewhere
- Stagnant high pressure :: light winds over a wide area :: regional accumulation, no direction to the transport
- Heavy rain :: precipitation scavenging :: removal of particulate, little effect on gaseous nitrogen dioxide
PARAM: INTERVENTION
- Traffic restriction by number plate :: a legal scheme and enforcement :: reduction of vehicle emissions within a day, no effect on any other source
- Solid fuel burning ban :: enforceable local rules :: reduction of domestic smoke, compliance difficult to verify indoors
- Industrial abatement order :: a permit condition and a named operator :: reduction at one stack, no effect on diffuse sources
- Public health advisory only :: a communication channel :: behaviour change by vulnerable people, no reduction in concentration at all
PARAM: OUTCOME
- Limits not exceeded :: concentrations remaining within standards :: no exceedance to report
- Short exceedance :: standards breached for a day or two :: reporting obligation, no lasting health signal
- Prolonged episode with excess mortality :: high concentrations sustained for a week :: measurable excess deaths, political consequence
- Attribution dispute :: sources contested between parties :: regulatory paralysis, no action taken
