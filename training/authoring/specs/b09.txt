DOMAIN: harmful algal bloom in a water reservoir
GROUP: environment
PARAM: BLOOM DRIVER
- Agricultural nutrient runoff :: fertiliser reaching the catchment watercourses :: diffuse origin with no single responsible party, loading concentrated after rainfall
- Sewage treatment discharge :: a consented outfall to the reservoir catchment :: a single identifiable operator, continuous rather than event-driven loading
- Internal phosphorus release from sediment :: anoxic conditions at the reservoir bed :: loading with no external input at all, unresponsive to any catchment measure
- Prolonged warm still weather :: an extended heatwave over the water body :: stratification and surface accumulation, no change in nutrient supply whatsoever
PARAM: BLOOM CHARACTER
- Surface cyanobacterial scum :: buoyant colonies accumulating at the surface :: visible discoloration, wind-driven accumulation on one shore, toxin production possible
- Dispersed subsurface growth :: cells distributed through the water column :: no visible surface sign at all, detectable only by sampling
- Toxin-producing strain :: a strain carrying the toxin genes :: hepatotoxin or neurotoxin in the raw water, a public health rather than aesthetic problem
- Non-toxic nuisance species :: a bloom-forming species without toxin genes :: taste and odour complaints, no health risk of any kind
PARAM: MONITORING
- Routine laboratory sampling :: a scheduled sampling round and a laboratory :: species identification and toxin assay, results days later
- In-situ fluorescence probe :: an instrument moored in the reservoir :: continuous pigment concentration, no species or toxin information
- Satellite surface imagery :: cloud-free conditions and a large water body :: mapping of surface accumulation extent, blind below the surface
- Public complaint :: someone noticing and reporting :: rapid unstructured signal, only for visible or smellable blooms
PARAM: RESPONSE
- Change abstraction depth :: multi-level offtake structures :: withdrawal below a surface accumulation, no help against a dispersed bloom
- Activated carbon dosing at treatment :: dosing capacity and contact time :: removal of toxins and taste compounds, cost and sludge burden
- Algicide application to the reservoir :: regulatory permission and a treatment vessel :: rapid cell kill, release of intracellular toxin as the cells lyse
- Catchment nutrient controls :: landowner agreement and years of time :: durable reduction of loading, no effect on the bloom under way

DOMAIN: level crossing collision
GROUP: transport safety
PARAM: CROSSING TYPE
- Automatic half barrier :: train-actuated barriers over half the carriageway :: a deliberate gap allowing escape, reliance on road user compliance
- Full barrier with obstacle detection :: barriers across the full width and a detector :: no gap to drive through, the signaller or system confirming the crossing is clear
- Open crossing with signs only :: sight lines and passive signage :: no barrier or light of any kind, total reliance on the road user looking
- User-worked gate on a farm track :: gates opened by the user :: the user judging the approach themselves, telephone to the signaller in some cases
PARAM: ROAD USER ERROR
- Zigzagging round a half barrier :: a gap wide enough to pass :: a deliberate violation, no equipment failure involved
- Misjudging train speed at an open crossing :: an approaching train visible but its speed misread :: an honest error, sight lines adequate in principle
- Vehicle stalling on the crossing :: a mechanical breakdown at the worst place :: no violation and no misjudgement, the vehicle immobile
- Long low vehicle grounding :: a vehicle whose profile fouls the crossing deck :: a predictable geometric incompatibility, no driver error in the moment
PARAM: PROTECTIVE SYSTEM
- Obstacle detection radar :: an installed detector and an interface to signalling :: signals held at danger when the crossing is occupied, only where fitted
- Train protection with automatic braking :: trackside and onboard equipment :: enforced braking at a signal at danger, no ability to stop within sighting distance
- Signaller closed-circuit television :: a camera view and a signaller watching :: confirmation the crossing is clear before releasing the signal, dependent on attention
- No protective system :: nothing beyond the crossing itself :: the driver of the train seeing the obstruction as the only defence
PARAM: CONSEQUENCE
- Near miss reported :: the conflict resolved without contact :: an incident report, no damage
- Road vehicle struck, no derailment :: a light vehicle displaced by the train :: road user casualties, train damage only
- Train derailed :: a heavy vehicle or grounding derailing the train :: potential mass casualties among passengers
- Crossing closed after the event :: a decision to remove the risk permanently :: no further exposure, road diversion cost

DOMAIN: offshore wind farm installation delay
GROUP: construction
PARAM: DELAY CAUSE
- Installation vessel unavailability :: a specialist jack-up vessel on charter :: a global fleet of very few suitable vessels, day rates continuing during any wait
- Weather window shortfall :: sea state limits for lifting operations :: seasonal, forecastable in aggregate but not in detail, no party at fault
- Seabed condition worse than surveyed :: ground truth differing from the geotechnical model :: foundation redesign, applies only to the affected positions
- Grid connection not ready :: onshore substation or cable works behind schedule :: turbines installed but unable to export, no offshore work affected at all
PARAM: FOUNDATION TYPE
- Monopile :: a single driven tube in suitable ground :: fastest installation, unsuitable in hard rock or very deep water
- Jacket structure :: a fabricated lattice with piles :: tolerance of difficult seabed and depth, long fabrication lead time
- Gravity base :: a heavy structure placed on a prepared bed :: no piling noise at all, very large dredging and ballast operation
- Floating platform :: a moored hull in deep water :: independence from seabed conditions entirely, quayside assembly and towing
PARAM: CONTRACT STRUCTURE
- Single turnkey contractor :: one party taking whole-scope responsibility :: a single point of accountability, the highest price
- Multi-contract package split :: several packages let separately :: lower price, interface risk carried by the developer
- Alliance with shared risk :: an agreement to share overruns :: aligned incentives, complex governance
- Owner-managed construction :: an in-house project organisation :: full control and full risk, dependence on scarce internal expertise
PARAM: PROJECT EFFECT
- Recovered within the programme :: float absorbing the delay :: no change to the commissioning date
- Commissioning slipped one season :: a weather window missed :: a year of lost revenue, financing cost
- Cost overrun without schedule impact :: additional resources bought to hold the date :: budget exceeded, programme intact
- Contractual dispute :: responsibility for the delay contested :: arbitration cost, relationship damage

DOMAIN: municipal solid waste disposal crisis
GROUP: public services
PARAM: DISPOSAL ROUTE LOST
- Landfill void exhausted :: the permitted void filled :: a permanent loss requiring a new permitted site, years to replace
- Incinerator outage :: a thermal treatment plant off line :: a temporary loss of tonnage capacity, weeks to months
- Export market closed :: a receiving country refusing refuse-derived fuel :: loss of an outlet with no domestic substitute, immediate and indefinite
- Recycling contamination rejection :: material quality below the specification :: rejection of collected material only, correctable by better sorting
PARAM: WASTE STREAM
- Residual household waste :: material after any separation :: continuous daily arisings that cannot be paused, public health consequence if uncollected
- Separated dry recyclables :: a clean sorted stream :: a market value when clean, storable for weeks without nuisance
- Food and garden waste :: putrescible organic material :: odour and vermin within days, no storage option at all
- Hazardous and clinical waste :: regulated material requiring specific treatment :: no substitution of route permitted, small tonnage and very high unit cost
PARAM: CONTINGENCY
- Divert to another authority :: a neighbouring facility with spare capacity :: continuity at higher haulage cost, dependence on a neighbour not also constrained
- Temporary storage on a licensed site :: a permit variation and a hardstanding :: days to weeks of buffer, odour and fire risk
- Suspend collections :: an operational decision :: immediate relief to the disposal system, waste accumulating at the kerbside
- Emergency permit for an alternative route :: a regulator willing to vary conditions :: a lawful outlet quickly, environmental safeguards reduced
PARAM: PUBLIC CONSEQUENCE
- No visible impact :: contingency absorbing the loss :: no consequence to residents
- Collections delayed :: rounds missed and rescheduled :: complaints, no health risk
- Waste accumulating in the street :: collections suspended for weeks :: vermin, odour and public health risk
- Environmental harm from disposal :: material handled outside normal safeguards :: pollution incident, regulatory enforcement
